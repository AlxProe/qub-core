QUB Core 1.8.6 / HF133 is available.

Mandatory mainnet upgrade: update before block #100000.
This applies to wallets, solo miners, pool miners and node operators.
Older releases, including 1.8.4, cannot follow the upgraded chain after activation.

HF133 activates ASERT-MTP difficulty adjustment at #100000, with a 60-second
target, to reduce repeated difficulty swings under changing mining power.
It also includes synchronization and memory improvements. A 60-second target
is not a guarantee for each block; mining power still affects block arrival.

Download: https://download.qubit-coin.io/

If the old Windows auto-updater closes the app without updating it: stop mining,
close QUB Core, download the installer from the official page and run it over
your existing installation. Reopen the app and confirm version 1.8.6.
Keep your existing wallet and chain-data folders.
